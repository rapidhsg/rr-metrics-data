##  Who We Are

Rapid Home Service Group builds home-service brands that scale, dominate, and stay profitable. Inside our organization, **marketing and advertising are two different functions**. Advertising is the distribution — the platforms we use to get our message in front of people, like Google, Meta, YouTube, LSAs, mailers, billboards, and any paid media. Marketing is the strategy behind the message itself — the offer, the positioning, the language, the framing, the visuals, the psychology, and everything that influences whether a customer takes action. Advertising delivers reach. Marketing drives conversion. Both matter, but they serve different roles in how we acquire customers at a cost that allows us to grow aggressively while staying profitable.

Our job is simple: **acquire customers efficiently, consistently, and predictably**.

## Our Core Beliefs

These principles guide how we operate marketing for every RHSG brand:

### Data beats opinion, always.

We do not rely on feelings, hunches, or vanity metrics. We operate on measurable performance indicators:

- Cost per lead

- Cost per appointment

- Customer acquisition cost

- Gross Profit

- CPV:CAC (customer profit value / customer acquisition cost)

- Lead-to-appointment rate

- Ad Spend

- Sales Revenue

- ROAS

The data tells the story. We follow the truth, not opinions.

### We mainly judge performance by gross profit, not revenue.

**  
GPPC: Our Core Profit Metric**

Revenue is vanity. Gross profit is the number that matters. In roofing, decking, and some home-service verticals, customers rarely buy twice. Because of that, we don’t use traditional “lifetime value.” Instead, we measure **Gross Profit Per Customer (GPPC)** — the actual dollars our company keeps after COGS on a single job. GPPC is the foundation of all marketing decisions. It tells us how much profit each acquired customer produces and how much we can afford to spend to get the next one.

**GPPC:CAC = Gross Profit Per Customer ÷ Customer Acquisition Cost  
**This ratio is the single most important metric in evaluating the performance of any marketing channel.

### We make long-term decisions, not emotional ones.

Marketing channels fluctuate. Some weeks are strong, some are slow. This does not change our standards or our evaluation method.

We judge channels only after:

- A full 6–8 week test window

- 20–30 qualified sits

- 10+ closed jobs

- Enough volume to establish real signal

We do not kill channels too early, and we do not scale channels prematurely. Decisions are made based on statistically meaningful data, not emotion.

### Speed, precision, and clean data matter.

We do not wait until month-end to fix attribution or clean data. We verify and correct data weekly so month-end reporting is accurate and reliable.

Messy data leads to bad decisions, and bad decisions waste money. We don’t tolerate either.

### Every channel is its own mini-business.

Each lead source functions like a small P&L. For every channel, we expect:

- Clear inputs

- Clear outputs

- Clear notes

- Clear patterns

- Clear changes

- Clear findings

If a channel performs well, we scale it. If it’s inconsistent, we stabilize it. If it’s inefficient, we optimize it. If it’s unfixable, we shut it down.

### Marketing’s job is not to “get leads.”

Marketing’s job is to **acquire profitable customers** at a cost that supports growth.

This means we track:

- Appointment rates

- CAC

- GPPC:CAC

- Margins

- Consistency

### Sales and marketing are one system.

These teams are partners — not opponents. Sales does not blame marketing for “bad leads,” and marketing does not blame sales for “bad close rates.”

Instead:

- Sales reports lead quality

- Marketing adjusts demand and targeting

- Both teams improve the system together

We honor each other’s roles because both sides drive revenue.

### We experiment with discipline.

New channels are tested only with:

- A defined 6–8 week runway

- Minimum sit volume

- Minimum job volume

- A fixed test budget

- Clear expectations

- Proper documentation

Experiments are structured and intentional — not random.

### Results matter more than ego.

If a channel we believed in fails, we shut it down. If a channel we doubted performs, we scale it. We don’t care who suggested a channel. We only care if it works.

Performance is the scoreboard.

### We compete with ourselves.

Our standard is continuous improvement. Month after month, we aim for:

- Better CAC

- Better GPPC:CAC

- Cleaner data

- More accurate attribution

- Smarter decisions

- Stronger channel performance

We’re building a machine — and machines get more refined with every cycle.

### Our Promise

We operate marketing in a way that is disciplined, measurable, scalable, repeatable, predictable, and profitable.

This philosophy guides how we work, how we make decisions, and how we win. Every RHSG brand will follow these principles so that — regardless of the service line — the framework stays the same:

**Acquire customers profitably.**
